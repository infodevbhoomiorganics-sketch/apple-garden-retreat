# Apple Garden Homestay Website

## What I’ll build
- A premium, mobile-first Himalayan homestay website with nine distinct pages: Home, Rooms, About, Amenities, Dining, Experiences, Gallery, Location, and Contact.
- A shared sticky navigation, mobile menu, consistent booking prompts, and a complete footer.
- Direct use of the ten supplied Apple Garden Homestay photos throughout the site, with no stock, generated, placeholder, base64, or remotely hosted imagery.

## Visual direction
- Warm boutique-retreat styling inspired by apple orchards, cedar forests, stone, mist, and Himalayan light.
- Cinematic photography, elegant editorial typography, restrained glass surfaces, subtle motion, and responsive layouts.
- The home page will feature the strongest mountain-and-balcony photograph as the full-screen opening image.

## Pages and interactions
- Home: cinematic introduction, stay highlights, atmosphere, and WhatsApp booking prompts.
- Rooms: truthful accommodation presentation without invented room categories or unconfirmed facilities.
- About: property story and host section naming Akhil Gautam.
- Amenities, Dining, and Experiences: only the confirmed offerings supplied in the brief.
- Gallery: categorized masonry presentation with zoom and fullscreen lightbox.
- Location: the exact supplied Google Maps embed, address, and directions action.
- Contact: phone, WhatsApp, directions, and an enquiry form that composes a WhatsApp message rather than claiming instant booking.

## Accuracy and discoverability
- Use the supplied phone number, address, rating, review count, and wording consistently.
- Add unique page titles, descriptions, Open Graph text, semantic headings, descriptive image text, clean page addresses, and LodgingBusiness structured data.
- Keep every booking and availability action linked to the supplied pre-filled WhatsApp enquiry.

## Technical details
- Build with the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind setup.
- Define reusable design tokens and shared site components, while giving each page distinct content and metadata.
- Import the supplied image files directly from the project assets folder.
- Verify the finished experience on desktop and mobile, including navigation, lightbox, enquiry flow, links, map, and preview errors.
